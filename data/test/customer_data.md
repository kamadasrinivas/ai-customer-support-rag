# Customer Support Knowledge Base

## Company Information

**Company:** NovaShop  
**Industry:** E-Commerce  
**Support Email:** support@novashop.example  
**Support Hours:** Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM IST

NovaShop is an online retailer selling electronics, home appliances, accessories, and personal technology products.

Customers can contact support for:

- Order status
- Shipping and delivery
- Returns and refunds
- Product issues
- Payments and billing
- Account problems
- Warranty
- Cancellations

---

# 1. Order Management

## 1.1 How can I check my order status?

Customers can check their order status from:

**My Account → Orders → Order Details**

The following statuses may appear:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Order Placed | Order has been successfully received |
| Payment Confirmed | Payment has been successfully processed |
| Processing | Warehouse is preparing the order |
| Shipped | Package has been handed to the courier |
| Out for Delivery | Package is expected to arrive today |
| Delivered | Package has been delivered |
| Cancelled | Order has been cancelled |

If an order remains in **Processing** for more than 48 hours, the customer should contact support.

---

## 1.2 Can I change my delivery address?

A delivery address can be changed only before the order is shipped.

Once an order has been shipped, the delivery address cannot normally be changed.

Customers should contact support immediately if the address is incorrect.

---

## 1.3 Can I cancel my order?

Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped.

After shipment, cancellation is normally not available. The customer may instead request a return after delivery if the product is eligible for return.

---

# 2. Shipping and Delivery

## 2.1 What are the standard delivery times?

Standard delivery usually takes **3–7 business days** after the order has been shipped.

Remote locations may require additional delivery time.

---

## 2.2 My order is delayed. What should I do?

Customers should first check the tracking information in their account.

If the package has not moved for more than **72 hours**, support should investigate the shipment with the courier.

The support agent should collect:

- Order ID
- Customer name
- Delivery address
- Tracking number
- Date of order

---

## 2.3 What should I do if my package says delivered but I did not receive it?

The customer should:

1. Check whether another family member received the package.
2. Check with neighbors or building security.
3. Verify the delivery address.
4. Contact NovaShop support.

Support should raise a delivery investigation with the courier.

---

## 2.4 Can I request express delivery?

Express delivery is available only for selected products and locations.

The availability and additional delivery charge are displayed during checkout.

---

# 3. Returns

## 3.1 What is the return policy?

Most eligible products can be returned within **30 days of delivery**.

Products must generally be:

- Unused
- In original condition
- Returned with original accessories
- Returned with original packaging where applicable
- Accompanied by the order information

Some products may have different return conditions.

---

## 3.2 Which products cannot be returned?

The following products may be non-returnable:

- Personalized products
- Digital products after activation
- Products damaged due to customer misuse
- Products without required accessories
- Products specifically marked as non-returnable

The product-specific return policy takes precedence over the general return policy.

---

## 3.3 How do I request a return?

Customers can request a return through:

**My Account → Orders → Select Order → Request Return**

Customers should select the reason for the return and provide supporting information if requested.

---

## 3.4 My product arrived damaged. What should I do?

Customers should contact support within **48 hours of delivery**.

They should provide:

- Order ID
- Description of damage
- Photographs of the damaged product
- Photographs of the package if available

Support will review the case and determine whether replacement, refund, or another resolution is appropriate.

---

# 4. Refunds

## 4.1 How long does a refund take?

After a return is approved and processed, refunds are normally initiated within **5 business days**.

The time for the amount to appear in the customer's bank account or card may vary depending on the payment provider.

---

## 4.2 Where will I receive my refund?

Refunds are normally returned to the original payment method.

For example:

- Credit card payment → Credit card
- Debit card payment → Debit card
- UPI payment → Original UPI-linked payment method
- Wallet payment → Original wallet

---

## 4.3 I received a partial refund. Why?

A partial refund may occur because:

- Only part of the order was returned.
- Some items were not eligible for refund.
- A promotional discount was applied.
- Shipping charges were excluded according to the applicable policy.

Support should review the order and refund calculation before providing a final explanation.

---

# 5. Payments

## 5.1 What payment methods are supported?

NovaShop supports:

- Credit cards
- Debit cards
- UPI
- Net banking
- Selected digital wallets

Available payment methods may vary by location.

---

## 5.2 My payment was deducted but the order was not created.

If money was deducted but no order was created, the customer should not immediately make another payment.

Support should collect:

- Customer name
- Payment reference number
- Transaction date
- Transaction amount
- Payment method

The payment status should be verified before creating or retrying the order.

---

## 5.3 I was charged twice for the same order.

Customers should contact support with:

- Order ID
- Transaction IDs
- Transaction amount
- Date and time of both transactions

Support should verify whether both transactions were successfully captured.

If a duplicate payment is confirmed, the duplicate amount should be processed for refund according to the payment investigation process.

---

# 6. Account Management

## 6.1 I forgot my password. What should I do?

Customers can reset their password using:

**Login → Forgot Password**

A password-reset link will be sent to the registered email address.

The reset link expires after **30 minutes**.

---

## 6.2 I cannot access my registered email address.

Customers who cannot access their registered email should contact support.

Support may request identity or account verification before changing account information.

---

## 6.3 How can I change my phone number?

Customers can change their phone number from:

**My Account → Profile → Contact Information**

Additional verification may be required.

---

# 7. Product Support

## 7.1 My product is not working.

Customers should provide:

- Order ID
- Product name
- Model number
- Description of the problem
- Error message, if any
- Photos or video if requested

Support should first check the troubleshooting instructions for the specific product.

---

## 7.2 My device is not turning on.

Customers should try the following:

1. Confirm that the power cable is properly connected.
2. Try another compatible power outlet.
3. Check whether the charging indicator is visible.
4. Follow the manufacturer's reset instructions.
5. Contact support if the problem continues.

Customers should not attempt internal repairs while the product is under warranty.

---

## 7.3 The product I received is different from what I ordered.

Customers should contact support immediately.

They should provide:

- Order ID
- Product received
- Product originally ordered
- Photograph of the received product
- Photograph of the shipping label

Support will investigate the issue and determine the appropriate replacement or return process.

---

# 8. Warranty

## 8.1 How long is the warranty?

Warranty duration depends on the product and manufacturer.

The warranty period is displayed on the product page and order documentation.

Customers should provide the order ID when requesting warranty assistance.

---

## 8.2 What does the warranty cover?

Warranty coverage generally applies to manufacturing defects.

Warranty may not cover:

- Accidental damage
- Water damage
- Unauthorized modifications
- Normal wear and tear
- Damage caused by misuse

The manufacturer's warranty terms take precedence.

---

# 9. Customer Complaints

## 9.1 I am unhappy with my order. How can I escalate the issue?

Customers can request escalation if the issue has not been resolved by standard support.

The escalation should contain:

- Customer name
- Order ID
- Issue description
- Previous support interactions
- Requested resolution

High-impact issues such as repeated billing errors, payment disputes, or unresolved delivery problems should receive priority.

---

# 10. Priority and Escalation Rules

## Priority Levels

### Low Priority

Examples:

- General product questions
- Delivery information requests
- FAQ questions

Expected handling: Normal support queue.

### Medium Priority

Examples:

- Delayed shipment
- Product troubleshooting
- Return request
- Account access issue

Expected handling: Standard support investigation.

### High Priority

Examples:

- Duplicate payment
- Payment deducted without order
- Incorrect product delivered
- Damaged product
- Repeated unresolved complaint

Expected handling: Prioritized investigation.

### Critical Priority

Examples:

- Suspected fraudulent transaction
- Multiple unauthorized transactions
- Significant payment dispute
- Security-related account compromise

Expected handling: Immediate escalation to the appropriate specialist team.

# 13. RAG Safety Rules

The RAG assistant should follow these rules:

1. Answer only using information retrieved from the knowledge base.
2. Do not invent policies, prices, refund amounts, or delivery dates.
3. If the knowledge base does not contain the answer, say that the information is unavailable.
4. Do not claim that a refund has been processed unless the customer/order system confirms it.
5. Do not expose internal customer information.
6. Escalate payment fraud and account-security issues.
7. For product-specific policies, prefer the product-specific policy over the general policy.
8. When two policies conflict, use the most specific applicable policy.
9. Do not provide a definitive resolution when additional verification is required.
10. Clearly distinguish between general policy information and information about a specific customer order.
